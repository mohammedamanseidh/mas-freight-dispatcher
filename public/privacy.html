Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

MAS Freight ("we", "our", or "us") operates the MAS Freight mobile application and website (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our Platform.

By registering and using MAS Freight, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register and use the Platform:

  • Identity information: Full name, national ID number, phone number
  • Vehicle information: Truck plate number, trailer plate number, truck type, expiry dates
  • Document images: Photos of driver license (front and back), national ID (front and back), truck photos (front and back), trailer photos (front and back), and Djibouti entry permit (where applicable)
  • Location data: GPS coordinates during active trips via Sinotrack integration
  • Financial information: Trip earnings, expense logs, and commission records (no bank or payment card data is collected)
  • Device information: Device token for push notifications

How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to operate the MAS Freight Platform:

  • Verify your identity and vehicle documents before approving your account
  • Match truck owners with available freight loads
  • Enable dispatchers to review and accept driver applications
  • Track active trips for operational coordination
  • Send you SMS verification codes and push notifications about your applications and trips
  • Maintain records of completed trips and earnings

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is shared only as follows:

  • Freight dispatchers: Your truck details, documents, and contact information are shared with MAS Freight dispatchers for the purpose of reviewing your application for specific loads
  • SMS providers: Your phone number is shared with Africa's Talking solely for OTP delivery
  • Cloud infrastructure: Your data is stored on Supabase (PostgreSQL) servers with encryption at rest

We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other commercial third parties.

GPS and Location Data

Location tracking via Sinotrack is activated only when you initiate a GPS session in the app using your own Sinotrack credentials. Location data during active trips is visible to the MAS Freight dispatcher for operational coordination only. We do not store GPS history on our own servers.

Document Images

Photos of your documents (license, ID, permits, truck) are stored securely in encrypted cloud storage and are accessible only to authorized MAS Freight dispatchers for the purpose of verifying your registration. These images are never shared publicly or used for any other purpose.

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request account deletion, your personal data and document images will be permanently deleted within 30 days. Trip financial records may be retained for up to 7 years for legal compliance under Ethiopian law.

Your Rights

Under Ethiopian data protection principles, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking by not using the GPS feature

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), encrypted storage, JWT-based authentication with expiry, and rate-limited OTP verification. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take all reasonable steps to protect your data.

Children's Privacy

MAS Freight is a professional logistics platform intended for commercial truck owners and operators. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under 18 years of age. If you believe a minor has registered, contact us immediately.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
